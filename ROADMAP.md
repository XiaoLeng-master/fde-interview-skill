# fde-interview Roadmap

This roadmap communicates direction rather than guaranteed delivery dates.
Changes must preserve evidence boundaries and candidate privacy.

## v0.1.x — Strengthen the first public release

- Maintain Chinese and English documentation.
- Add automated package and fixture validation.
- Improve routing consistency across Readiness Diagnosis, Roadmap, Mock
  Interview, and Case Drill.
- Add sanitized transition scenarios only after privacy and truthfulness review.
- Clarify scoring explanations and evidence-gap labels.
- Correct time-sensitive role and market references as sources change.

## v0.2.0 — Deeper practice and evaluation

- Expand industry-specific FDE Case Drills.
- Improve English interview mode and international platform-role overlays.
- Define a repeatable evaluation runner while preserving platform-neutral
  fixtures.
- Add score-consistency checks across repeated assessments.
- Add structured candidate progress and re-test guidance.

## Future direction

- A versioned, source-backed FDE job-description dataset.
- Change tracking for FDE, FDSE, Applied AI, and adjacent role expectations.
- A community library of sanitized delivery cases.
- Additional regional and industry overlays.
- Reusable evaluation reports for Skill maintainers.

## Evidence and privacy policy

A roadmap item is not a shipped capability. Examples must be sanitized, metrics
must have a source, and no customer, employer, candidate, or NDA-protected
information may be published without authorization.
